Hidden windows

In the switcher, AltH or a row's hide button keeps that window out of the list; Shift+click the button to hide every window of the app. A hidden window is recognized by its app and title, so it comes back if its title changes.

Your hints

Open apps automatic hints

Click a hint to change it. Hints are 1 to 4 letters or digits, and every window of the app shares them (two windows of an app with hint B get B1 and B2). Your hints can't start with each other: B rules out BA. Automatic hints rearrange around yours.
